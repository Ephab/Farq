# Waypoint demo video — plan

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, ~92 s, light "app-store / polished" tone with a calm, confident build.
**Angle:** follow one fictional student, *Layan Alharbi* (3rd-year AI, IAU), from "I just signed up" to
"everything about my degree is in one place, and an AI coach keeps it moving". Every frame is the real app,
recorded live against the local stack; AI waits are cut or fast-forwarded with an on-screen "sped up" chip.

**Hook (first 3 s):** the app's own portal intro ("Waypoint") resolving into the title line
"Your degree, mapped. Your coach, always on."

**Visual identity:** the app's own type (Bricolage Grotesque display, Instrument Sans body), monochrome
white UI, one dark pill chapter chip at the top centre of each scene, a visible cursor with click ripples.

**Persona & data (all fictional):** Layan's CV PDF and an "ARTI 404 Lecture 5: CNNs" deck are generated
for the video. Group Projects uses the bundled demo sandbox (four fictional teams), renamed to Layan in
the recording browser only. No real account, mailbox, Blackboard login or grades appear.
