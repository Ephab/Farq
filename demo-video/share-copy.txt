Waypoint: your degree, mapped. Your coach, always on.

Meet Layan, a third-year AI student. In about two and a half minutes she:
- signs up, syncs her Blackboard in one step, then imports her CV.
  Nothing becomes part of her profile until she ticks it.
- answers a few questions from Hermes, our AI coach, which only asks what her records didn't cover.
- gets a roadmap built live around her own projects and goals, where every topic lists real sources to learn from.
- changes the plan just by asking ("add a separate detection project on the same palm leaves"). Hermes drafts it; she accepts, and it appears on her roadmap.
- browses this term's Blackboard files in one slide library, and turns two lectures into a quiz.
- asks Hermes what a coursework email means for her: what to do, and by when.
- sees co-op matches with Saudi organisations.
- runs a group project: reads the team chat, accepts a Hermes proposal and starts a poll with a slash command.
- switches to Arabic, with a full right-to-left layout and 12 themes.
Also inside: learning updates, projects, a CV builder, a slide workbench, a "My data" view and a shared team server.
Hermes, her coach, can work with all of these features and her data.

Every frame is the real app running locally. AI wait times are sped up and marked on screen.
