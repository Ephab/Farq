# Waypoint: education problems and our solutions

Waypoint is a continuous student companion from the first day of university through graduation and
co-op. Hermes is the agent inside Waypoint. It learns from information the student confirms, provides
guidance, and proposes changes, while the student remains in control.

## 1. Students feel lost

**The problem:** A degree provides courses and deadlines, but students still struggle to understand
what to learn next, how different subjects connect, or how today's work supports a future goal.

**Waypoint's solution:** An adaptive, top-down roadmap combines university courses with the student's
interests, available time, progress, strengths, and chosen direction. It gives the student a clear
next step while keeping future branches flexible. Hermes can propose a revised path when the student
changes direction or demonstrates new skills, but completed and in-progress work is preserved and no
revision is applied without approval.

## 2. Academic curricula become outdated

**The problem:** Universities teach essential theory and fundamentals, but curricula cannot always
keep pace with new technologies, tools, practices, and changing industry expectations.

**Waypoint's solution:** The roadmap augments—not replaces—the curriculum with current industry
skills such as Git, Docker, system design, AI tooling, communication, and portfolio building. It can
also include current resources, projects, podcasts, books, hackathons, and events. Course context from
Blackboard can support explanations, quizzes, reminders, and updated learning material; the current
hackathon build uses a clearly identified read-only indexed snapshot, with official integrations left
for production.

## 3. There is a gap between university and industry

**The problem:** Knowing a subject academically is different from building, collaborating, shipping,
and proving that knowledge in a real working environment.

**Waypoint's solution:** Learning sequences lead to practical projects, and students can refine each
project with Hermes until it is something they want to build. Waypoint can evaluate submitted project
artifacts in an isolated environment and turn progress into evidence. Hackathon and Saudi co-op
recommendations then connect the student's demonstrated skills and interests to real opportunities,
while showing preparation gaps before they apply.

## 4. Students become overdependent on AI

**The problem:** A general chatbot can finish work for a student without ensuring that the student
understands it, practices it, or takes ownership of the result.

**Waypoint's solution:** Hermes is designed as a coach and collaborator. It gives concise explanations,
practice questions, exercises, and selectable learning branches instead of silently replacing the
student. In group projects, Hermes acts as a teammate: it helps divide work according to each member's
ability and growth needs, assists discussion, and proposes changes for the team to accept. The roadmap
can also explicitly teach effective and responsible use of tools such as coding agents.

## 5. Learning tools lack personal context

**The problem:** Students repeatedly upload the same files and explain their background to disconnected
tools. Advice is often generic because it does not know what they have already studied, built, or chosen.

**Waypoint's solution:** Waypoint maintains an auditable student profile containing confirmed courses,
skills, projects, goals, decisions, progress, and feedback. Hermes uses that context to explain new
topics in terms the student already understands. Imported evidence remains unconfirmed until the
student reviews it, and the application database—not agent memory—is the source of truth.

## 6. Career preparation starts too late

**The problem:** Students often begin looking for co-op placements near the deadline, without knowing
which organizations fit their interests or which skills they still need.

**Waypoint's solution:** A dedicated co-op experience matches confirmed skills, projects, and goals to
Saudi companies and current postings. Hermes explains each match, identifies missing preparation, and
can turn those gaps into roadmap actions so the student becomes a stronger candidate before applying.

## The central idea

Waypoint does not replace the university, the teacher, or the student. It connects them. University
content provides the foundation, current opportunities keep the plan relevant, Hermes provides
continuous guidance, and the student makes the final decisions.
