Waypoint · "خط واحد" (One Line) · cinematic pitch, Arabic, ~5 min

Open index.html in Chrome or Edge. Everything is local (fonts, GSAP, images, screen recordings).
If the browser blocks local files, serve the folder:  python -m http.server  and open http://localhost:8000
Press F for fullscreen.

Next  : Right / Down / PageDown / Space / Enter / left click (presenter clickers work)
Back  : Left / Up / PageUp / Backspace / right click
Home / End : first / last beat
N     : speaker notes + beat counter + timer (timer starts on the first press)

Concept: the road cut through the Waypoint wordmark is one living line. It draws itself into the logo,
gets pulled out and tangles (lost), splits between 2019 and 2026, flatlines at "وبعدين؟", shatters under
seven apps, rejoins as one mint road, carries the ten product waypoints, flows through the architecture,
and flies back into the logo for the close.

12 scenes / 51 beats:
 1 cold open (one press: the line draws the logo)    8 convergence: seven apps become one
 2 third year, still lost                            9 product: 10 steps along the road (12 beats)
 3 four problems                                    10 group projects (7 beats)
 4-7 the four problems, one scene each              11 how it works
                                                    12 closing: back into the logo, thanks
