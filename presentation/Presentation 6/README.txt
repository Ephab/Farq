Concept
- The opening dot IS the i-dot of the Waypoint logo; it leaves the logo and draws the thread.
- The camera rides a smoothed rail of the thread (gentle travel, no fast dot animation).
- After the "too many apps" slide the 10 app chips fly into one glowing point over the i. The road
  ends exactly at the free tail of the logo's own line; the dot then glides along that line while the
  logo is drawn from the tail, and lands on the i (burst).
- Finale: a flat map of the whole journey (its own SVG, no giant 3D zoom): it starts close on the
  end of the road and pulls back to show a grey scribble (the lost student) that finds the Waypoint
  dot, then one clean road through the eight stations.
- Hermes proposes, the student decides: imported evidence stays "مقترح" until approved.
